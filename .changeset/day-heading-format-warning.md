Warn when day headings are written in a different date format from the kind's day-heading format, for example `## 10.05.2026` with `YYYY-MM-DD`. Before, activities under those headings silently showed as not on a day. The trip tab lists the affected notes under Needs attention, and the kind's settings show the mismatch with a button to use the format the headings are written in. Notes are never changed. The day-heading format setting now previews today's full heading, for example `## 2026-11-01`.
